# Try Scrollcase in your browser

This scenario starts a real disposable Linux environment and prepares a published Scrollcase demo box for you.

The setup runs automatically in the background:

- installs the Scrollcase CLI;
- downloads the published Linux x86_64 CPU demo;
- downloads its trusted public key separately;
- leaves the box untouched and ready to verify and run.

No local installation, Pixi, Conda or conda-pack is required.

Click **START** to continue.
